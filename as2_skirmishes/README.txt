AS2 Classic Skirmishes — Call to Arms: Gates of Hell Mod
=========================================================

Included Missions (Singleplayer -> AS2 Skirmishes):
  1. Seelow Heights (Germany vs. USSR — Spring)
  2. Kursk (USSR vs. Germany — Summer)
  3. Kharkov (Germany vs. USSR — Winter)
  4. Bocage (USA vs. Germany — Normandy; requires Liberation DLC)

Installation:
  1. Extract the `as2_skirmishes` folder into:
     <SteamLibrary>\steamapps\common\Call to Arms - Gates of Hell\mods\
     (so you have `...\mods\as2_skirmishes\mod.info`)
  2. Launch Call to Arms - Gates of Hell.
  3. Go to Extras -> Mods -> activate "AS2 Classic Skirmishes" -> Apply.
  4. Open Singleplayer -> AS2 Skirmishes.
